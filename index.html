The kitchen

We cook with
Texas post oak.

The same wood that built Central Texas barbecue burns in our oven every night. It burns hot and clean, and it leaves a little smoke in every crust — a quiet nod to the city we cook in.

Our dough rests for two full days before it meets the fire. That slow cold ferment is where the flavor comes from: a crisp, leopard-spotted edge, a tender middle, and a crust you'll actually finish.

No heat lamps, no conveyor belts. Just a dome of brick, a pile of oak, and a crew who watches every pie like it's the only one.

Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Do you take walk-ins?

Always. The bar and patio are first-come, first-served, and we hold a few tables each night for walk-ins. Reservations are the surest bet on Fridays and Saturdays.

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

Yes. Any pizza can be made on our gluten-free crust for $3 extra. It's baked in the same oven, so we can't promise it's safe for celiac guests. The Marinara is vegan as-is, and we're happy to swap in vegan cheese.

How big are the pizzas?

Each pie is about 12 inches. One per person is a hearty dinner; two pizzas and a small plate is just right for a couple.

Can I book for a large group or private event?

We seat groups of up to 12 through the reservation form. For bigger parties or a full buyout, call us and we'll put something together.

Is there parking?

There's free street parking on the surrounding blocks and a paid lot across the street. Bike racks are out front.

Are kids and dogs welcome?

Kids, absolutely — we have high chairs and a plain cheese pie that never fails. Well-behaved dogs are welcome on the patio.
